RollPass Log Privacy Policy

App Store name: RollPass Inc. Log. Effective October 11, 2026.

RollPass Log is a jiu-jitsu training journal for iPhone. It is built to work entirely on your device. It has no account, no servers, no analytics and no advertising. This policy covers the RollPass Log iOS app only.

Data the app uses

  • Apple Health. With your permission, the app reads workouts, heart rate and active energy to fill in your training sessions. It never writes to Apple Health. Health data is not used for advertising or marketing, and is not shared with anyone.
  • Location. With your permission, the app uses your location to set up up to three gyms and to notice when you arrive at and leave them. Location is not recorded as a history and never leaves your iPhone.
  • Microphone and speech recognition. When you tap record, the app records a voice note of up to two minutes and transcribes it on your iPhone. Audio is deleted after transcription unless you turn on Keep audio in Settings.
  • Apple Intelligence. On supported devices, the transcript is summarized into partners, techniques, wins and problems by Apple's on-device model.
  • What you enter. Sessions, effort ratings, notes, gyms and profile settings.

Where your data goes

Nowhere. Everything is stored on your iPhone. The app sends no data to the developer or to any third party. Searching for a gym and showing the map use Apple Maps, an Apple system service covered by Apple's privacy policy. Your app data is included in your own iPhone backups (iCloud or computer), which you control.

Notifications and Siri

Notifications asking you to confirm a session are created on your iPhone. If you use the Siri or Shortcuts action, it runs on your iPhone like any other in-app entry.

Deleting your data

Settings > Delete all data removes every session, gym, voice note and setting the app has stored, and stops location monitoring. Deleting the app also removes its data. You can revoke Health, location, microphone and speech access at any time in the iOS Settings app.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because it collects no information at all.
